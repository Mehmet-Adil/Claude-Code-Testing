Our Story

Started in a garage with a popcorn popper.

Ember & Bean began in 2019 when two friends got tired of stale supermarket coffee. What started as roasting beans in a secondhand popcorn machine turned into a tiny roastery obsessed with one thing: flavor.

Today we work directly with twelve farms across four continents, roasting every batch by hand. No shortcuts, no stale beans — just honest coffee.
